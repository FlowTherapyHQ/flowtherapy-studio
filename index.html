Wellness Made Routine.

Make therapeutic massage part of your routine. FlowPass includes one personalized Signature Flow Bodywork session each month, plus member benefits that make ongoing care easier to maintain.

Each Signature Flow Bodywork session is tailored to your needs, comfort, and pressure preferences. No separate deep tissue charge. Eligible Recovery Lab tools included.

Choose your session length

Your membership includes

  • One Signature Flow Bodywork session each month.
  • $20 off additional massages.
  • $10 off CBD Calm and TriFlow Muscle Recovery.
  • One complimentary birthday FlowBoost. Time Boost is excluded.
  • One unused session may roll over into the following membership cycle.
Membership details & terms

Commitment: Three-month minimum, then membership continues month to month.

Rollover: One unused session may roll over and must be used by the end of the following cycle.

Cancellation: After the initial commitment, cancellation requires 30 days’ notice.

Pauses: Membership may be paused for up to three months, up to twice within a 12-month period.

Personal membership: Memberships and monthly sessions are for the enrolled member only and cannot be transferred or shared.

Regular appointment cancellation and no-show policies apply. Time Boost is excluded from the FlowBoost discount and birthday benefit.

Ready to make time for yourself?

Choose your session length, then select Start My Membership. FlowTherapy will follow up with your enrollment agreement and payment arrangements. Your membership begins once enrollment, payment, and activation are completed.

Meet Your FlowTherapist

Care for the whole person.

Massage is healthcare—not a luxury.

Hi, I’m Brandy Hennigan, LMT—a trauma-informed therapeutic massage therapist, lifelong student of human movement, and firm believer in the healing power of touch.

I work with people experiencing persistent tension, stress, headaches, movement restrictions, recovery needs, and the everyday wear and tear life places on the body.

I believe care should be collaborative. You’re encouraged to ask questions, communicate what you need, and move at a pace that feels right for you. Every session is tailored to your goals because no two bodies—or wellness journeys—are the same.

My goal is to help you move with greater ease, build resilience, and feel more at home in your body.
