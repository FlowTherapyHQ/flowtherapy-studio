Before you arrive

A smoother session starts with a little preparation.

The written introduction in your guide explains why forms and timing matter, then the checklists offer simple reminders for before, during, and after your massage.

Forms · Before your first massage

Complete your consultation forms ahead of time.

Texas TDLR requires an initial consultation and a signed consultation document before your first massage. Completing FlowTherapy's forms before your appointment also gives your therapist time to review your goals, relevant health information, and preferences so the session can be prepared for you. Read TDLR's consultation guidance.

Timing · Your appointment

Please arrive on time.

Appointments are scheduled closely. If you arrive late, your massage may need to be shortened so the next appointment can begin on time. Your session will still end at its scheduled time.

Communication · During your massage

Your comfort matters.

You can ask for a pressure adjustment, a position change, a quieter session, or a different focus at any time. Your feedback helps your therapist adapt the session to you.
