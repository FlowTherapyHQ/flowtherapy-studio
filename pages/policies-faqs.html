FlowPass Membership

Understand your membership and its benefits.

What is FlowPass?

FlowPass is FlowTherapy's membership offering. Review the current membership details and benefits before enrolling.

Explore FlowPass →

Can I transfer my FlowPass membership?

No. FlowPass memberships are nontransferable.

Can I receive a refund for FlowPass?

FlowPass memberships are nonrefundable. Please review the applicable membership terms before purchasing.

Can I pause my membership?

Membership pauses are subject to the FlowPass terms, including the permitted pause frequency and the effect a pause has on membership benefits. Contact FlowTherapy if you need help understanding your membership status.

What if my membership payment is declined?

FlowPass has a seven-day grace period for a declined payment. Membership benefits may be suspended when payment is paused. Please contact FlowTherapy to resolve billing questions.

What discounts do members receive on enhancements?

Eligible FlowPass members receive a $10 discount on qualifying FlowBoost enhancements, including CBD Calm and Tri-Flow. Time Boost is excluded from this discount.

Care, Intake & Consent

Informed care starts with clear communication.

Why do I need to complete an intake form?

Intake information helps your therapist understand your goals, relevant health information, and preferences so your session can be planned appropriately.

Complete your client forms →

How is my comfort and privacy respected?

Please communicate your preferences and boundaries before and during your session. You may ask questions, request an adjustment, or withdraw consent at any time.

For information about the handling of personal information, review the privacy information provided by FlowTherapy.

Does massage replace medical care?

No. Massage is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. Consult an appropriate healthcare professional about medical concerns, and share relevant information with your therapist before your session.
