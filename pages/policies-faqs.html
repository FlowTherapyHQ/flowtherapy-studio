Booking & Scheduling

Everything starts with knowing what to expect.

How do I book a FlowTherapy appointment?

New clients must book online. This lets us reserve your first appointment and collect the intake and consultation information we need to prepare for your session. Returning clients can text 737-387-FLOW for help choosing a service or with a booking question.

Explore available appointments →

Which massage service should I choose?

If you’re new or unsure where to start, try Focused Flow. The 45-minute upper-body option is a comfortable introduction and can focus on your neck, shoulders, and upper back. For full-body work, choose Signature Flow. Compare all options on our services page; returning clients can text 737-387-FLOW for help choosing.

When am I charged for my appointment?

FlowMobile appointments are paid in full when you book. For studio appointments, a card on file is required; some longer services also require a $50 deposit, which is applied to your session total and shown during booking.

Can I book a FlowTherapy Studio appointment?

FlowTherapy Studio appointments are not available to book just yet. A new studio is coming very soon, and we’ll share its location and booking details when they’re ready. FlowMobile is currently bookable online in Downtown Austin.

Explore FlowMobile →

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. For FlowMobile, you may reschedule for free or request a full refund with at least 24 hours’ notice; cancellations within 24 hours are nonrefundable. For studio appointments, cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice and missed appointments forfeit the deposit. See the full Booking & Cancellation Policy.

What if I arrive late?

Late arrivals may shorten your session so the next appointment can begin on time. Your session will still end at its scheduled time. Please contact FlowTherapy as soon as you know you may be late.

Your Experience

Your comfort, preferences, and boundaries matter.

What should I do before my first appointment?

Complete the appropriate intake and consent forms before your session, and share relevant information about your goals, preferences, and any concerns you would like your therapist to consider.

How should I prepare for a FlowMobile session?

Set aside a clear floor area of about 6 × 10 feet. Your FlowTherapist brings the heated table, fresh linens, and music. For a hotel, office, or apartment, include the property name and any parking or entry instructions with your booking.

Explore FlowMobile →

Where can I book FlowMobile?

Online bookings are currently available in Downtown Austin. Expanded service across Austin and Central Texas is coming soon; the locations listed on the FlowMobile page show the planned service area.

View FlowMobile service areas →

Are FlowMobile travel fees or deep-tissue surcharges extra?

No. FlowMobile’s listed prices include the $50 mobile service and travel fee, with no additional travel or deep-tissue surcharge at checkout. Hot towels, hot stones, glide cupping, and Rapid Flow Release with a massage gun are complimentary options for your session. Your full booked session is hands-on massage time.

See FlowMobile options →

What should I wear, and how does draping work?

Undress only to the level that feels comfortable for you. You’ll remain appropriately covered with a sheet throughout the session, with only the area being worked on uncovered. You can ask for a change or pause at any time.

Can I book back-to-back FlowMobile sessions?

Yes. You can request consecutive appointments for two or more people during one visit. Contact FlowTherapy so we can confirm timing and availability for your location.

Explore FlowMobile →

Can I request lighter or firmer pressure?

Yes. Please communicate your pressure preferences and let your therapist know if anything feels uncomfortable. You can ask for an adjustment at any time during your session.

Can I change my mind during a session?

Absolutely. You may communicate a change in preference, request an adjustment, or ask to pause or stop the session at any time.

FlowPass Membership

Understand your membership and its benefits.

What is FlowPass?

FlowPass is a monthly wellness membership with one 75- or 90-minute Signature Flow session each month, $20 off additional massages, and $10 off qualifying FlowBoosts. One unused session may roll over into the following cycle.

Explore FlowPass →

What is the FlowPass commitment and how do I cancel?

FlowPass has a three-month initial commitment. After that, membership continues month to month and can be canceled with 30 days’ notice.

Review FlowPass terms →

How does FlowPass session rollover work?

One unused monthly session may roll over and must be used by the end of the following membership cycle.

Review FlowPass terms →

Can I transfer my FlowPass membership?

No. FlowPass memberships are nontransferable.

Can I receive a refund for FlowPass?

FlowPass memberships are nonrefundable. Please review the applicable membership terms before purchasing.

Can I pause my membership?

Membership pauses are subject to the FlowPass terms, including the permitted pause frequency and the effect a pause has on membership benefits. Contact FlowTherapy if you need help understanding your membership status.

What if my membership payment is declined?

FlowPass has a seven-day grace period for a declined payment. Membership benefits may be suspended when payment is paused. Please contact FlowTherapy to resolve billing questions.

What discounts do members receive on enhancements?

Eligible FlowPass members receive a $10 discount on qualifying FlowBoost enhancements, including CBD Calm and Tri-Flow. Time Boost is excluded from this discount.

Do you accept HSA or FSA cards?

Yes. FlowTherapy accepts HSA and FSA cards through Noterro. Your plan determines whether a massage expense is eligible for payment or reimbursement. We can provide an itemized receipt; check with your plan administrator about eligibility and any additional documentation they require.

Is tipping expected?

Tipping is never expected and is always appreciated. Your FlowTherapist will never ask you for a tip.

Can I purchase an enhancement without a massage?

Some enhancements have standalone pricing, while others are designed to accompany a massage service. Check the current service menu or contact FlowTherapy to confirm the options for a particular enhancement.

Care, Intake & Consent

Informed care starts with clear communication.

Why do I need to complete an intake form?

Texas TDLR requires an initial consultation and a signed consultation document before your first massage. Completing FlowTherapy’s forms before your appointment also gives your therapist time to review your goals, relevant health information, and preferences so your session can be prepared for you.

Read TDLR’s consultation guidance →

Complete your client forms →

How is my comfort and privacy respected?

Please communicate your preferences and boundaries before and during your session. You may ask questions, request an adjustment, or withdraw consent at any time.

For information about the handling of personal information, review the privacy information provided by FlowTherapy.

Does massage replace medical care?

No. Massage is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. Consult an appropriate healthcare professional about medical concerns, and share relevant information with your therapist before your session.

What if I’m sick or have a health concern?

Please contact FlowTherapy if you have a fever or contagious illness; do not attend while contagious. If you have a health condition, recent surgery, or are unsure whether massage is appropriate, check with your healthcare professional and share relevant information with your therapist before your session. If illness affects an appointment within 24 hours, contact us promptly to discuss next steps.
